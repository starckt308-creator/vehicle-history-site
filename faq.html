Frequently asked questions

Answers about this website prototype and what is needed to launch live reports.

Are these real vehicle history results?

No. This is a front-end demo. It does not check live vehicle databases or official registries.

What is a VIN?

A Vehicle Identification Number is a vehicle identifier, commonly 17 characters for modern road vehicles, although formats can vary. Verify it against the vehicle and its documents.

Can the site check cars in every country?

Not yet. Country coverage depends on the data providers, permissions, and supported identifiers connected to the site.

Does the support chat use AI?

Not in this starter version. It uses simple demo replies. A real AI assistant needs a backend connection and appropriate safeguards.

Is payment enabled?

No. The pricing page is informational only and no payments are processed.

Can I use the report to decide whether to buy a car?

No. Demo data is fictional. For an actual purchase, check official records and get an independent inspection.
